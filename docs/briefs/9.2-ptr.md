# Brief 9.2: retire `Ptr<T>` from the engine and the rules

Milestone 9.2 of `docs/milestones.md`, the first hand rewrite of the second stage. The units in
`src/Units` are hand-edited source since the hand-over (brief 9.0). `Ptr<T>` is the first stage's
model of a Delphi pointer (`docs/specs/rtl-shim.md` section 1.4, `docs/specs/transpiler.md`
section 5.8): a backing array and an index, boxed when it escapes. Read `docs/design.md` section 9
for the rules of the second stage.

You work in `D:\git\open-aow` on the branch the prompt names, and in `D:\git\open-aow-private`
under `tools` and `tests`. Read both `AGENTS.md` files.

## What to do

1. **Inventory** every use of `Ptr<T>`, `PtrBox<T>` and the pointer helpers in `src/Units`, by unit
   and by pattern (a walk over an array, a typed view of a buffer, a cell for a `var` address, a
   list's backing store, a string's characters), and write it to
   `D:\git\open-aow-private\data\rewrite\ptr.md`.
2. **Rewrite, unit by unit**, in the units of Engine, HSEngine and the rules (AoWEPACK, AoWTCPCK,
   aowFXpck, Network): a walk over an array becomes indexing or a `Span<T>`, a cell for a `var`
   address becomes a `ref` or the field itself, a list's backing store becomes the list's own
   storage, a typed view becomes a struct or an explicit conversion at the boundary. Each unit is
   its own commit-sized step, with `python tools/check.py` passing after it (at least the product,
   probes, oracle and frames layers between steps, the whole check at the end).
3. **What stays.** The graphics and image library code (GFXE, ImageLib, `src/Graphics`) keeps
   `Ptr<T>` until milestone 9.5, as the milestone says. So do byte strings, which milestone 9.5
   replaces.

The behavior does not change: the oracle, the frames and the scenarios are the proof. A rewrite
that changes a saved byte or a pixel is wrong until shown otherwise.

## Done measure

`Ptr<T>` no longer appears in Engine, HSEngine and the rules units (a command that counts its uses
by unit shows zero outside the graphics and image library code), `python tools/check.py` passes
every layer with the same counts, and the solution builds with zero warnings. Report the inventory
before and after, the patterns and how each was rewritten, and anything left with its reason. Keep a
status file at `D:\git\open-aow-private\tools\obj\9.2-status.md`.
