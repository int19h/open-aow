# Brief 7.1: from the title scene to the first turn of a new game

The first step of milestone 7 of `docs/milestones.md` (a player starts a scenario and plays it).
The title scene and nine in-game frames already equal the original's pixel for pixel (milestones
3.4 and 3.5), and the rules play the original's game turn for turn (milestone 2.4). This brief
connects them through the menus a player uses: the title scene, the single-player menu, the
scenario selection, the game setup dialog with its player and race choices, and the first turn of
a human player's game on the map.

You work in `D:\git\open-aow` on the branch the prompt names, and in `D:\git\open-aow-private`
under `tools/oracle` and `tests/frames`. Read both `AGENTS.md` files, `docs/specs/graphics.md`
section 9, `docs/specs/ui-layout.md` sections 4 and 7, and `tests/frames/report.md` for how the
capture works and what it had to fix.

## What to build

1. **A scripted path** through the menus, as a fixture script both sides replay: from the title
   scene, choose single player, choose a scenario (Mountain Clash), keep the setup dialog's
   defaults except for a human first player, start, and play the first human turn far enough to
   select a unit, move it one hex and end the turn. Clicks and key presses at recorded frames.
2. **New reference frames** from the original with the capture tool, one per screen of the path
   (the menu, the scenario list, the setup dialog, the first map frame of the human turn, the unit
   selected, the unit after its move, the frame after end turn), each captured twice with identical
   pixels, under the native-run rules below.
3. **The port's side**: every screen of the path renders through the fixture mode and equals its
   reference with no masks, and the game state after the human turn (an autosave or an explicit
   save at the end of the script) equals the original's under the oracle comparator. Every code
   path the menus and the human turn reach that hits a TODO gets its override or transpiler fix,
   with a test or probe that pins it.

## Rules for native runs on this PC

Native runs go through the copied harness on its hidden desktop, with sound and music volume 0,
the game's registry key verified restored after every run, processes stopped only by the PID the
harness recorded, and an immediate stop if a window or dialog appears on the user's desktop.

## Done measure

Every new fixture equals its reference with no masks, the state after the human turn equals the
original's, `python tools/check.py` passes with the new fixtures in its frames layer, and the TODO
count is reported before and after. Report the fixtures, the fixes with what pins each one, and
anything not done. Keep a status file at `D:\git\open-aow-private\tools\obj\7.1-status.md`.
