## What to build

1. **`src/Headless`**, a console project in the product solution, as `testing.md` 3.2 step 3 and
   `platform.md` 10 describe: `--load <save> --seed <n> --turns <n> --out <dir>` (and
   `--tactical` as section 3.1 describes it). It loads through the game's load path, forces
   `RandSeed` and makes `Randomize` a no-op, calls `AoWHSMap.Activate` (Delphi `TAoWHSMap`), then calls
   `AoWHSMap.NewFrame` in a loop, and at every `NewDay` writes `AutoSave` to
   `<out>/day-NN.asg`, until the turn count or `TriggerGameTerminatedEvent`. Null display and
   sound, a scripted dialog service that answers the default button and logs each dialog, the fixed
   clock, the display-state viewport of `testing.md` 3 (0x0 until the map view is first shown,
   then 1600x940). The hooks are host services or overrides, never edits of generated code, and
   the seed interventions are the two the debugger makes, no more.
2. **`tools/oracle/run.py` and `compare.py`** in the private repository, as section 3.2 steps 1,
   2 and 4 describe, with the JSON result format of `compare_runs`
   (`D:\git\aow\tools\playtest.py`, read it). `compare.py` reads a pair of runs or a run and the
   original's reference directory.
3. **Fixes.** Every TODO the run reaches becomes an override or a transpiler fix, every exception
   a bug fixed at its cause. Keep the list: what, cause, fix.
