The next step of milestone 7, with the method of briefs 7.1 and 7.2: scripted paths, every screen

## The paths

1. **A tactical battle won entirely by hand**: every unit moved and every attack chosen by the
   script, through to the victory screen and back to the map, with the state after the battle.
2. **A campaign start**: the campaign menu, choosing a campaign and a side, its briefing, and the
   first map frame of the first campaign scenario.
3. **Heroes and items**: open a hero's window, move an item between heroes or to the inventory,
   and the hero's statistics after it.
4. **A diplomacy action**: propose a treaty from the relations window and the response frame.
5. **Recruiting and building**: buy a unit in a city and build a structure, across the turn end.

## Rules for native runs on this PC

Native runs go through the copied harness on its hidden desktop, with sound and music volume 0,
the game's registry key verified restored after every run, processes stopped only by the PID the
harness recorded, and an immediate stop if a window or dialog appears on the user's desktop.

`python tools/check.py` passes. A path that cannot be made equal is reported with the first
differing frame or state and its cause. Report the paths, the fixes with what pins each one, the
TODO count before and after, and anything not done. Keep a status file at
