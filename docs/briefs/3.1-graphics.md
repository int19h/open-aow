   every stub override replaced by the real routine.
3. Blend tables of section 4.
4. The `blitters` probe (`tests/probes/blitters.pas`, a DCC32 program that uses `GFXE` and

   routine and format, section 5.2) and the `blendtables` probe, recorded under DCC32 like the
   other probes. Their C# side runs the same battery against the port's routines. Extend the
   runner only to let these two probes link the recovered units.
