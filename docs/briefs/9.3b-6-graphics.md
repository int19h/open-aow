## Scope

Every class with a split initializer or a guarded static factory in `src/Units/ImageLib.cs`,
`src/Units/GFXE.cs`, `src/Units/GFXE.MemorySurfaces.cs`, `src/Units/DisplayC.cs` and its partial
files, and their descendants in `src/Platform`. List them first in the status file with their
bodies and the shape the rule gives each.

- **Plain:** the body only assigns fields, allocates private children, or calls virtual methods
  whose overrides do the same.
- **Context-guarded:** the body publishes the object (registers it with a palette, a list, a
  cache, a global) or acquires something its `Destroy` releases, and later work on the chain can
  throw. `ConstructionContext` is internal to `Aow.Rtl` and `Aow.Units` already sees it.
- **Not converted:** anything that fits neither cleanly (work before the base body, a skipped base
  initializer, reentry on an existing object, a raw `NewInstance` consumer such as the isometric
  `Attach` path). Leave such a class as it is, list it in the report with the line and the reason,
  and continue with the rest. Do not stop the run for it.

Keep ordinary virtual calls at their current positions and `base.X()` calls that stand for Delphi
`inherited` as non-virtual calls. Do not move work into field initializers. A parameterless
constructor stays only for a named raw consumer. Callers inside your worktree use `new` or the
guarded factory. Callers in the private repository are listed in the report with a ready patch.
Do not change `src/Units/Engine.cs` or `src/Units/AoWE.cs` beyond caller lines: other runs edit
their bases.

context-guarded class: a failure after its publication or acquisition leaves the same external
state (palette registrations, lists, reference counts) as today, pinned on main first. The frame
fixtures cover drawing.
