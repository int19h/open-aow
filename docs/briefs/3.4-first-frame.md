## What to build

1. **`SdlDisplayController`** as section 7.3 specifies, behind `IDisplayHost` of section 8, on the platform's SDL
   host: the back surfaces, `BltSurface` and `FlipSurface` presenting through the streaming 555
   texture, the scaling and fullscreen policy of section 7.4, `GetMousePos` through the scale, the
   cursor handling of section 7.5.
2. **A headless presenter**: an `IDisplayHost` whose `Present` writes the frame as `frame-<n>.raw`
   (top-down 555) with a JSON of size and frame number, as section 9 step 3 says, so that a fixture
   renders without a window. Every automated check uses it: no window opens on the user's desktop
   during a check.
3. **The frame loop and startup**: `src/Game` starts as `AoW.dpr` does through `TMainForm`
   (`platform.md` section 2): the 77 modules in the layout's creation order, `CustomDisplay` (Delphi `TCustomDisplay`) with
   the main form's settings, the synchronous loop of section 7.2 with the frame limiter on `IClock`,
   `DisplayUpdateFrame`, and the title scene drawn by `General.Manager.DrawSurface`. Sound stays
   silent (milestone 5.2).
4. **The frame comparison**: `tools/oracle/compare_frames.py` of section 9 step 3 (pixel count,
   first differing pixel, a PNG with the differences highlighted, the fixture's masks), and a mode of
   `src/Game` that renders a fixture through the headless presenter: the title scene at frame 60
   with the fixture's settings from `tests/frames/fixtures.toml` once brief 3.5a has written it.
