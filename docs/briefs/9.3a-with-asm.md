# Brief 9.3a: `with` locals to plain code, and the assembler shapes A1 to A3

Milestone 9.3 of `docs/milestones.md`, first part. The constructors are the second part (brief
9.3b), which needs this one merged first. Read `docs/design.md` section 9 for the rules of the
second stage, `docs/specs/transpiler.md` section 5.2 for how `with` was emitted and its section on
assembler bodies (rules A1 to A3) for the shapes.

You work in `D:\git\open-aow` on the branch the prompt names, and in `D:\git\open-aow-private`
under `data\rewrite` and `tools\obj`. Read both `AGENTS.md` files.

## What to do

1. **Inventory.** Before any edit, list every `with` local (`w__N`, 569 declarations in 91 files,
   56 of them `ref var`) by unit and by the shape of its expression, and every routine of
   `D:\git\open-aow-private\data\asm-routines.json` with `kind` `constant` (35), `thunk` (165) and
   `allocator` (1), with where its C# body is now. Write it to
   `D:\git\open-aow-private\data\rewrite\with-asm.md` with a command that recounts it.
2. **`with` locals.** Each `{ var w__N = E; ... }` block becomes plain code:
   - When `E` is a name that cannot change inside the block (a parameter, a local or a field
     that the block never assigns, `this`), the uses of `w__N` become `E` and the local goes.
   - Otherwise the local stays, because Delphi evaluates `E` once (a call, a property, an indexed
     element whose index or list changes in the block, a cast). It gets a name that says what it
     holds (`army`, `unit`, `field`), unique in its method.
   - A `ref var` local over a record stays a `ref` local with a real name, so that writes still
     reach the original. A copy of a record from a call or property stays a copy.
   - The extra braces go when no name clashes.
3. **Constants (A1).** A constant body becomes an expression body, `public virtual int
   GetAbilityID() => -1;`, keeping its `// Unit.pas:line` comment.
4. **Thunks (A2).** 162 of the 165 thunks were added by the recovery and have no counterpart in
   the original binary (`transpiler.md`, the assembler table). Where a thunk is a plain static
   routine, its callers call the target directly, the thunk goes, and so does its `{ needs: ...
   Tail jumps ... }` comment. A thunk stays, as an expression body that calls the target, when
   it is virtual or overrides, is assigned as an event handler or method value, is named by a
   form or layout, or is called from outside `src/Units`. The inventory says which and why.
5. **Allocator (A3).** `MapField.Meta.NewInstance` (Delphi `TMapField`) only calls the base and goes, and so does
   `MapFieldMemory` when nothing else reads it. The class comment keeps the note that the
   original pooled the fields of a level in one block.

Each unit, or a group of small units, is a step, with at least the product, probes, oracle and
frames layers of `python tools/check.py` passing after it and the whole check at the end. Do
not touch the constructors (`Create__init`, the factories, `Meta.Create`), the `Meta` types or
the strings: those are briefs 9.3b, 9.4 and 9.5.

The behavior does not change: the oracle, the frames and the scenarios are the proof. A rewrite
that changes a saved byte or a pixel is wrong until shown otherwise.
