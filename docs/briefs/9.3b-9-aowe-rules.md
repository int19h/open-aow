## Scope

Every class with a split initializer or a guarded static factory in `src/Units/AoWE.cs` and its
partial files (`AoWE.*.cs`) that does not descend from `Troop` (Delphi `TAbstractUnit`): combat actions, production
items, abilities, items, cities and their lists, players, the AI and event objects, the game set and
map containers, and everything else in those files. The `Troop` hierarchy is a later brief.
Brief 9.3b-7 (merged into your base) already gave the spell, combat-action and ability bases their
semantic constructors, so convert their remaining AoWE descendants on top of them.

List every class in scope first in the status file with its bodies and the shape the rule gives
it.

- **Plain:** the body only assigns fields, allocates private children, or calls virtual methods
  whose overrides do the same.
- **Context-guarded:** the body publishes the object (owner insertion is already done by
  `EngineObject`, so this means a further list, cache, global or registry) or acquires something its
  `Destroy` releases, and later work on the chain can throw.
- **Not converted:** anything that fits neither cleanly (work before the base body that the base
  observes, a skipped base initializer, reentry on an existing object, a raw `NewInstance`
  consumer). Leave such a class as it is, list it in the report with the line and the reason, and
  continue with the rest. Do not stop the run for it.

The design note names ordering exceptions to keep: `AoWEngine` (Delphi `TAoWEngine`) (it fills `Registry` and
publishes its global before the base body, whose virtual `UpdateStartupDirectory` reads it),
`EventLog` (Delphi `TEventLog`)-like snapshots before owner callbacks, and `CombatMap` (Delphi `TAoWCombatMap`)'s global ordering. Leave
those as they are and list them. `ArmyHexSprite` (Delphi `TArmyHS`) has a partial destructor that dereferences `Army`.
Keep its failure behavior and say what it is. `AIMoveControlSettings` (Delphi `TAIMoveControlSettings`) calls virtual `Clear` and
`MoveSelector` (Delphi `TMoveSelector`) calls virtual `CreateMoveControlSettings` after their fields are set. Keep those calls where they
are and check the overrides read only what is already set. `CombatMoveControlSettings` (Delphi `TCombatMoveControlSettings`) allocates
owned children in its inherited body and the design note asks to keep its cleanup.

Keep ordinary virtual calls at their current positions and `base.X()` calls that stand for Delphi
`inherited` as non-virtual calls. Do not move work into field initializers. A parameterless
constructor stays only for a named raw consumer. Callers inside your worktree use `new` or the
guarded factory. Callers in the private repository are listed in the report with a ready patch.
A static factory with a `new` modifier that no longer hides anything loses the modifier. Do not
add warning pragmas.
