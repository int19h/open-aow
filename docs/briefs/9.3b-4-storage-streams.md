## The family and the decisions

The root is `EStorageStream` (Delphi `TEStorageStream`) (`src/Units/Engine.cs`, near line 5839), a `TObject` with its own
hierarchy. It is not registered in `MetaRegistry.cs`. All classes below get plain constructors
except `DebugWriteStream` (Delphi `TDebugWriteStream`):

- `EStorageStream`: `(TStream AStream, Engine AEngine)`. Its body only assigns fields.
- `EReadStorageStream` (Delphi `TEReadStorageStream`) with `SetupCompareStorageStream` (Delphi `TSetupCompareStorageStream`) and `CompareStorageStream` (Delphi `TCompareStorageStream`), and
  `EReadTextStorageStream` (Delphi `TEReadTextStorageStream`). Their bodies read and parse the borrowed stream after allocating
  private tables and lists. A read that throws leaves a partly built reader that nobody references.
  It owns nothing outside itself, so the collector reclaims it. Today's guarded factory runs
  `Destroy` on it, which only frees those private children. Dropping that is not an observable
  change. `CompareStorageStream` keeps passing `partial = false`, and `SetupCompareStorageStream`
  keeps the three-argument signature it inherits today.
- `EWriteStorageStream` (Delphi `TEWriteStorageStream`), `EWriteTextStorageStream` (Delphi `TEWriteTextStorageStream`) with `AoWE.ExportTextStream` (Delphi `TExportTextStream`), and
  `TranslateStorageStream` (Delphi `TTranslateStorageStream`) with `AoWE.TranslateTextStream` (Delphi `TTranslateTextStream`). Their bodies only assign fields and
  allocate private buffers and tables, so under the allocation clause their construction does not
  fail. The writers' `Destroy` writes to the caller's stream. That output only follows a
  successful construction and does not change. State this in the report with the lines.
- `DebugWriteStream` keeps its split initializer, as the survey's group 4 says. It deliberately
  skips the writer's initializer, borrows the supplied buffer, and its cast of that stream can
  throw. `EWriteStorageStream` therefore keeps a protected raw constructor for it, named with
  its consumer, and the debug initializer and factory stay exactly as they are. Add it to
  `ctor-progress.md` as `retained` with that dependency and its exit condition (an explicit
  debug-writer constructor proven against a fixture of its failure output).

If a body does something these decisions do not cover (a publication, a virtual call on the
object being built, an acquisition of something outside the object), stop and report it with the
line.

   initializers and their `// Unit.pas:line` comments. The initializers and static factories go,

3. Private callers outside your write paths are listed in the report with a ready patch, as the
